# Resume audit, 2026-11-23

Scope (frozen): whether resuming a member (`worker run`, any checkpoint kind, OOM retries included) changes its training computation; whether code changes `e9baab8..HEAD` change the computation of an uninterrupted member; and what `long-cot`'s artefacts show around its resume at step 475. Invariant checked: for every step S, a resumed member's adapter (fp32) and full AdamW state (both moments, step counter and hyperparameters of every parameter) before the update, and after it given the same rollouts, equal bit for bit those of an uninterrupted member. Code read at `HEAD` = `92e7ec5`; `member.py`, `trainer.py` and `worker.py` are unchanged since `26e7575`, the revision that wrote long-cot's step-475 light checkpoint.

Scripts (Compute project, throwaway): `runs/audit/resume_audit.py`, `runs/audit/resume_negative.py`, `runs/audit/optimizer_chain.py`. Run with `source scripts/env.sh`, CPU only.

## Q1. Resume from each checkpoint kind reproduces the update exactly: holds

Code path. A checkpoint is written by `Member.write_checkpoint` (`evotaste/member.py:427-435`): fp32 adapter via `save_adapter(..., float32)` and `torch.save(weights.optimizer)` when one exists. It is committed by `state.json` (`member.py:386`, `411`); `prune_checkpoints` (`member.py:415-425`) removes `optimizer.pt` only from checkpoints other than the committed one. `restore` (`member.py:214-238`) loads exactly the committed step's directory: `load_adapter` (fp32, `trainer.py:193-199`) and `load_optimizer` (`trainer.py:298-299`). `update` (`trainer.py:241-266`) rebuilds AdamW, loads a private CPU copy of the state, resets `lr` and `weight_decay` from the current settings, and writes weights and state back only after `opt.step()`. The step-0 checkpoint has no `optimizer.pt`, matching `seed()` setting `optimizer = None` (`member.py:259`), so both paths start amplification with a fresh AdamW.

Test (`resume_audit.py`). Drives the real `worker.advance` with real `Member`, `Policy`, `retry_on_oom`, checkpoint and restore code on a tiny Qwen3 (CPU), two members (one with a per-member `lr` override, one perturbation arm), `--checkpoint-every 5`, probes every 10, `truncation_penalty` 0.5, 23 steps. A fake sampler serves each version from its bf16 hand-off file and makes rollouts a pure function of the prompt, so both scenarios see identical rollouts; a fake scorer replaces the scorer pool. Scenario A runs uninterrupted. Scenario B is five launches, each with a fresh `Policy` (fresh model copy) and state only from disk, killed (a `BaseException`, like SIGKILL) during step 3 (resume from the step-0 checkpoint, fresh optimizer), during step 8 (from the light checkpoint at 5), after writing the step-15 light checkpoint but before committing `state.json` (from the probe checkpoint at 10), and during step 21 (from the probe checkpoint at 20). Seven CUDA OOMs were injected and retried: in a seeding `AdamW.step` after the parameters had changed, in the consistency check, after backward, and inside `AdamW.step` during amplification.

Result: all 46 (lineage, step) updates identical before and after the update (adapter and every optimizer tensor, `torch.equal`); the 24 redone steps reproduce their first attempts; histories (loss, grad_norm, sequences, tokens, reward, groups) identical; every checkpoint adapter byte-identical; final `optimizer.pt` identical. Before the update B's optimizer is fresh at step 0 and has AdamW step = S at S = 3, 5, 8, 10, 15, 20, 21, with the member's own `lr` (1e-3; 3e-3 for the override). Negative controls (`resume_negative.py`): forcing a fresh optimizer at resume, or loading bf16-rounded weights, makes the same comparison fail from the first affected step, so the test is sensitive to both faults.

Not covered: GPU kernels (the CPU→GPU copies in `_activate` and `load_state_dict` are exact copies; GPU backward nondeterminism affects uninterrupted runs equally).

## Q2. No path to a fresh or stale optimizer, other hyperparameters, another stream position or bf16 weights: holds, with one silent fallback

- Weights: `restore` reads `checkpoints/step_S/adapter_model.safetensors`, written in fp32 (`member.py:431`); the bf16 copy goes only to `/dev/shm` for vLLM (`member.py:262-267`) and is never read back. The fp32 round trip is bit-exact (`test_adapter_files_roundtrip`, and byte-identical checkpoints above). Long-cot's step-480 checkpoint, the first written after the resume, is fp32 with only 0.001% of values having zero low 16 bits; a bf16-derived copy would have 100%.
- Optimizer: on live data, `optimizer_chain.py` compares the AdamW step counter in each lineage's newest `optimizer.pt` with the number of amplification updates with active sequences in its history. All 40 lineages of `main1-tp` match: long-cot 615 = 615 after 8 resumes, and `terse-b` 28 = 28 (two empty steps). A fresh or stale state at any of the night's resumes would show a smaller counter.
- Hyperparameters: `lr` comes from the frozen run config plus member overrides on every update (`member.py:200`, `306`; `trainer.py:256-257`); launch flags change only server settings and checkpoint frequency (`worker.py:88-100`, `335-376`). Config defaults added since `e9baab8` (`truncation_penalty` 0, `check_tokens`) do not change existing behaviour (`git diff e9baab8..HEAD -- evotaste/config.py`).
- Stream position: `step_tasks` uses the committed `lin.step` (`member.py:274-275`) and the stream is a pure function of (seed, step) (`tasks.py` `draws`). Steps after the checkpoint are dropped from history and rollouts (`member.py:225-232`) and redone with fresh rollouts (documented: sampling is unseeded). No step is applied twice; the step counters above confirm it.
- Defect (low; no evidence it occurred): `load_optimizer` returns `None` when `optimizer.pt` is missing (`trainer.py:298-299`), so a resume from a step-S>0 checkpoint whose file was lost would silently restart AdamW, against the fail-fast principle. Smallest fix: in `restore`, raise when S > 0, the file is missing, and the history up to S has an update with `sequences > 0`.

## Q3. Changes `e9baab8..HEAD` that alter an uninterrupted member's computation: none beyond documented ones

Changes to the data or rewards, all predating `main1-tp`'s preparation at `b255f69` and therefore common to all its members: `724c86b` (instances fixed under `PYTHONHASHSEED=0` and seeded global `random`; changes instances of hash- or `random`-dependent families relative to `e9baab8`), `02ae5db` (scorers in killable processes with a 10 s wall-clock bound instead of SIGALRM), `bb9f158` (truncation penalty; `main1-tp` uses 0.5), `26e7575` (redraw of generator-failing items; before it the worker crashed at those positions, so no member trained on the old item).

Changes with no effect on the update: `15a5995` (consistency-check sample size and confirmation; it only gates, `logprobs` re-activates nothing the update relies on); `6f5d00f` (private optimizer copy and OOM retry; computation identical, tested above, and no OOM retry occurred in any `main1-tp` worker log); `bf7a24e` (4 CPU threads; the forward, backward and AdamW run on the GPU, and the CPU work is exact dtype conversion and copies); the bf16 LoRA autocast path is unchanged since `e9baab8` (`git show e9baab8:evotaste/trainer.py`, line 203); `8dc16ce`, `528d933`, `cef3ea4`, `ce34b99` are timing, server, transport-retry and harvest changes.

Provenance gap: worker logs record a revision but not the uncommitted diff. Five worker launches ran from dirty trees. Four committed nothing that later runs continued from. One did: pid 1239413 at `26e7575-dirty`, 00:04–07:00 on 23 November, wrote the checkpoints at steps 25–90 of `verify-b`, `backward-a`, `enumerate-b`, `formalize-a`, `state_table-b`, `terse-a`, `cot-2`, `perturb-1`, `cot-5`, `cot-6` (and `terse-a~1` steps 5–60), which later workers resumed from. What was modified cannot be established. The commits made within minutes touch only `scripts/supervise_run.sh`, `docs/design/protocol.md` and `evotaste/evaluate.py`, which the worker does not import, and the optimizer chains of these lineages are intact. Smallest fix: `worker run` refuses a dirty tree, or logs a hash of `git diff` at start. Long-cot never trained under a dirty tree that committed anything.

## Q4. Long-cot at steps 470–510: no sign of a resume artefact at 475

- Log: the worker (`26e7575`) wrote the step-475 light checkpoint at 03:26:13, ran steps 475–479, and was stopped by SIGTERM at 03:30:28 during the step-480 probe. The worker at `ce34b99` resumed from `step_000475` at 07:03:16. Neither log has an OOM, consistency-repeat or other warning besides dataset-cache notices.
- History at 470–474 compared with 475–479 (first steps after the resume): grad_norm 0.18, 0.23, 0.19, 0.18, 0.24 against 0.24, 0.17, 0.18, 0.16, 0.19; loss −0.004…−0.014 against −0.002…−0.008; consistency check token mean |Δ| 0.006–0.011 against 0.007–0.012, per-token sum 0.0013–0.0030 against 0.0008–0.0020; reward 0.39–0.41 against 0.37–0.41. Nothing is discontinuous at 475.
- What changes later: completion length falls from about 410 tokens (470–480) to 290–350 (486–496), then rises to 500–550 at 503–509, while truncation rises from 0.00–0.02 (470–500) to 0.04–0.10 (503–509) and groups with mixed rewards from 4–16 to 16–27. Reward is 0.35–0.44 up to step 497, 0.31–0.35 at 498–502, and 0.24–0.36 at 503–520. Rollouts have no scorer errors or timeouts at any of steps 400–599. Probe train accuracy: 0.383 (480), 0.399 (495), 0.302 (510), 0.274 (525).
- Files: the step-475 checkpoint was pruned after the step-480 commit, as designed. The current checkpoint's `optimizer.pt` (279,241,083 bytes, about 2 × 34.9 M fp32 values) has AdamW step 615, matching history.

## Impact

(a) Long-cot collapse: resume is not a plausible cause. The resume restores the exact state (Q1, Q2), the live optimizer chain is intact, the metrics show no break at 475, and the decline starts about 25 steps later, with a length swing that precedes it. (b) Comparisons between members resumed different numbers of times: resumes add no systematic difference. Redone steps draw fresh rollouts, which is the same sampling noise as any run. The remaining caveat is provenance: the 10 member lineages trained at steps 20–90 under one unrecorded dirty tree.
